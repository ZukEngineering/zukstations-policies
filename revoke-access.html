How to disconnect ZÜK Stations from your YouTube account

You can stop ZÜK Stations accessing your Google/YouTube account at any time, in either of two ways.

In ZÜK Stations

  1. Open Settings → Connected accounts.
  2. Next to the channel, choose Disconnect.
  3. ZÜK Stations revokes its permission with Google and deletes the data it holds about that channel within 7 days.

In your Google Account

  1. Go to https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions and sign in.
  2. Find ZÜK Stations (or the name of the Google Cloud project you created for it) in the list of apps with access.
  3. Choose it, then Remove access.

After this, ZÜK Stations can no longer read your channel's analytics or upload to it. To delete the data it had already stored on your computer, disconnect the channel in ZÜK Stations too, or delete its data folder.
