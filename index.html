Privacy Policy for ZÜK Stations

Effective: 28 September 2026 · Operator: ZukEngineering · Contact: admin@zukunftengineering.com

ZÜK Stations is desktop software that runs on your own Windows computer. It helps you research, produce and prepare videos for YouTube channels that you own or manage. This policy explains what information it uses, where it keeps it, and your choices.

1. We use YouTube API Services

ZÜK Stations uses YouTube API Services to read public YouTube data and, when you connect a channel, to read that channel's analytics and to upload videos to it as private. By using the YouTube features of ZÜK Stations you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. What information is accessed and why

Information Where it comes from Why
Public video and channel data (titles, descriptions, public statistics, public thumbnails, public comments) YouTube Data API, with an API key Trend research and learning which formats work in a niche
Your connected channel's details, videos, analytics and reports YouTube Data, Analytics and Reporting APIs, with your permission (OAuth) Showing how your videos perform, and improving future videos
Upload permission for your connected channel YouTube Data API, with your permission Uploading finished videos as private. You publish them yourself
Your OAuth refresh token Google, when you connect a channel Staying connected without asking you to sign in each time

ZÜK Stations does not ask for, collect or store your Google password.

3. Where information is kept

Everything stays on your computer:

  • Data is kept in the ZÜK Stations data folder you choose.
  • OAuth tokens and API keys are kept in Windows Credential Manager.

ZÜK Stations stores and reads this information on your device. It does not use cookies or tracking technologies, and it has no telemetry.

ZÜK Stations does not send your YouTube data to the operator or to anyone else. It is not sold, shared or used for advertising. The only outside services involved are:

  • Google/YouTube, which you connect to.
  • Optional services you switch on yourself, each under its own terms. For example, an image-generation service receives the prompts you send it.

4. How long information is kept

  • Data about channels you don't own, including their video and channel IDs: at most 30 days, then it is deleted automatically.
  • Your own channel's analytics and reports: kept while your channel stays connected. The connection is re-checked at least every 30 days. Other data from your channel is refreshed or deleted within 30 days.
  • Disconnecting a channel revokes its permission and deletes the data ZÜK Stations holds about it within 7 days.

5. Your choices

  • Revoke access at any time:
    • in ZÜK Stations: Settings → Connected accounts → Disconnect
    • or in your Google Account: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. See How to revoke access.
  • Delete data: disconnect the channel, or delete the ZÜK Stations data folder. If you ask us to delete data held for you, we act within 7 days.

6. Children

ZÜK Stations is not intended for children and does not knowingly process children's personal information.
